Every evening

Shake — don’t skip this

One Athletes Nutrition tray is not a full dinner. This shake is how you close most of the calorie and protein gap. Mix:

  • 1 scoop whey (~120 kcal, ~24–25 g protein)
  • 400 ml full-cream milk (~260 kcal, ~13 g protein)
  • 1 banana (~105 kcal)
  • 50 g rolled oats (~190 kcal, ~7 g protein)
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter (~95 kcal, ~4 g protein)

About 770 kcal and ~50 g protein, around $3 once you have a tub of whey. Estimate — check the tub and milk label

On a bigger dinner night (Nando’s or a GYG enchilada), a smaller shake of just whey and milk is enough, about 380 kcal and 37 g protein. Estimate
